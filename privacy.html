Privacy Policy for Tasker AI

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Tasker AI is built to provide seamless, privacy-respecting task management across devices. We do not sell, monetize, or share your personal information with third parties.

2. Google Drive Access & Data Storage

Tasker AI utilizes the Google Drive https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope strictly to store and synchronize your encrypted backup state (tasker_ai_backup.json). This file is stored exclusively within your private Google Drive application folder. Tasker AI does not access, read, or modify any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

3. Authentication

Authentication is handled via Google OAuth strictly to verify your account identity and facilitate Google Drive data synchronization.
